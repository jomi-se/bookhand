## Verified content

The package identifies the work as *Calculus Made Easy* by Silvanus P.
Thompson and declares `Public domain in the USA.` Its EPUB 3 navigation points
Chapter X, “GEOMETRICAL MEANING OF DIFFERENTIATION,” to
`OEBPS/7731425094544341602_33283-h-17.htm.xhtml#CHAPTER_X`.

Chapter X contains selectable XHTML narrative text and 19 referenced figures
with captions and descriptive alternative text. Mathematical notation is
mostly referenced SVG imagery rather than selectable glyph text; 220 equation
images in the chapter carry both `alt` text and `data-tex` source. The reader
must preserve those images and their accessible names when applying book CSS.

The approved UI mock uses illustrative Chapter X prose and a figure that do not
match this current official EPUB. Product implementation should render the
book's real text and figures rather than reproduce the mock's sample content.
