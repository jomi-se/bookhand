Chapter XIX · Finding areas

Strips into area

The area under a curve is hard to get at in one go. Cut it into strips of width dx, add them up, and let the strips get thinner.

strips
sum of y·dx
true area
S = \int dS = \int_{x_1}^{x_2} y\,dx The secret of solving this problem is to conceive the area as being divided up into a lot of narrow strips, each of them being of the width dx.
