Warm-up: from bits to ratios

◷ five minutes · four steps

You've got the bits. Now we compare them.

  • d means “a little bit of”. You own this.
  • A bit of a bit, (dx)², can be thrown away. Clicked yesterday.
  • dy⁄dx is a ratio, not a bit. Still wobbly: that's tonight.

1 · Two bits at once

Chapter III grows two things together. Nudge x by a bit, dx, and y moves by its own bit, dy.

Calculus doesn't care how big either bit is. It cares how they compare: dy / dx.

2 · Remember the corner?

Same move as the bit of a bit: we'll throw tiny things away once we've taken the ratio.

3 · Fig. 4, alive

A 30° triangle grows. Drag for dx and watch the ratio.

dy ⁄ dx

It never moves. On a straight slope the ratio is the same at every size: 1 ⁄ 1.73.
