Chapter I · The preliminary terrors

Two dreadful symbols, defused

Thompson's first move is to take the scariest symbols in the book and read them out in plain English. Tap each one.

tap d “a little bit of”
tap “the sum of”

∫ is just a long S, for Sum. Mathematicians say “the integral of”, which means “the whole”.

An hour is 3,600 little bits

Cut one hour into 12

…you are now to perform the operation (if you can) of totalling up all the little bits that are indicated by the symbols that follow. That's all.
