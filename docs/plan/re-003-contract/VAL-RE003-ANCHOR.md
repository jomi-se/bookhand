# VAL-RE003-ANCHOR: Window changes do not move the visible passage

Surface: browser.
Needs: VAL-RE003-WINDOW.
Behavior: loading or retiring content before the viewport compensates its size so continuous forward and backward traversal has no blank gap or material passage jump; flow switching does not move the reader to an unrelated section.
Evidence: visible text/range samples immediately before and after backward insertion, forward eviction, and backward eviction, plus section identity across Pages/Scroll switches.
