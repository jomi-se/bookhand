# 04 — Notebook and markings

Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Goal

Everything about a book is easy to find, both from the page and from one
place.

## Scope

- The Notebook replaces the Study panel: artifacts, highlights, memories, and
  conversations for this book, grouped by chapter, with type filters and a
  recent view. Book-wide artifacts get their own group. Dismissed items are
  reachable without cluttering the default view.
- Markings on the book in the direction chosen in 01, leading to their
  artifacts; each Notebook entry leads back to its anchor.
- Static previews in lists; live artifact frames only when opened.
- Works docked on desktop and on a phone.

## Done when

On the owner's phone, with a chapter holding several artifacts, highlights,
and a memory, the owner can find any of them from the page and from the
Notebook in a couple of taps, and returning to the exact passage is reliable in
Pages and Scroll mode.
