# VAL-TUTOR-STUDY-REVEAL: Guidance can reveal durable study content

Surface: study renderer, tutor controller, genuine WebMCP, and browser.
Needs: `VAL-TUTOR-SESSION-LIFECYCLE` and `VAL-STUDY-COMPOSITION-HIERARCHY`.
Behavior: `reveal_study_item` accepts exactly `{ bookId, itemId }`, where `itemId` names a current-book lesson or standalone item. Its exact outcomes are `applied|superseded|rejected|unavailable`, each with the resulting `guidance`; unknown, missing, or conflicting fields are rejected. A missing, cross-book, or stale item maps to `rejected` and changes nothing; no open/ready book or Study surface maps to `unavailable`; generation loss maps to `superseded`. A valid call opens Study transiently without changing the stored docked/expanded preference, scrolls the exact target into view, and focuses its meaningful heading. Lookup, panel opening, scrolling, and focusing capture runtime generation, book identity, and target ID; stale work cannot reopen Study, scroll, or steal focus after takeover/yield, new guidance, Back, Stop, close, detach, book change, or reload. Back restores the prior panel, location, and focus when valid; otherwise it focuses the stable Library toolbar button and reports that fallback truthfully. Stop leaves the currently visible panel in place, clears guidance, and leaves durable content unchanged.
Evidence: Serialized exact schema/result union; genuine calls for lesson and standalone item; unknown/missing/conflicting fields; geometry, scroll, heading focus, Back restoration and named focus fallback, Stop panel behavior, stored-preference, `rejected` missing/cross-book/stale and `unavailable` surface cases, mobile replacement, and reload traces; deterministic delayed lookup/open/scroll/focus races across every terminal transition.
