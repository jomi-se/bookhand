# VAL-MOBILE-GESTURES: Intentional pagination without selection theft

Surface: Chromium touch emulation with reduced-motion variants.
Needs: `VAL-READER-NAV`, `VAL-READER-SELECTION`, and production preview.
Behavior: A tap moves at most 10 CSS pixels over at most 350 milliseconds. When no panel or text selection is active, a tap whose start is in the left 25 percent of the book host goes to the previous page, the right 25 percent goes to the next page, and the center 50 percent toggles secondary chrome; these zones behave the same whether chrome starts shown or hidden. Taps on interactive EPUB/app controls do not page or toggle chrome. Drag intent locks only after 12 pixels and only when horizontal movement is at least 1.5 times vertical movement. A locked page turn completes at 20 percent of viewport width or 0.5 CSS pixels per millisecond in the intended direction and otherwise cancels; vertical/diagonal gestures do not page. Text-selection interaction is not treated as paging. Completion/cancellation snaps for 160–240 milliseconds with easing; reduced motion uses no animated snap.
Evidence: Programmatic touch traces at, below, and above every distance/time/ratio/velocity boundary; completed/cancelled/diagonal/vertical cases; selection-preservation case; animation duration/computed behavior; reduced-motion case. Native Android long-press remains an explicit non-gating open target under ADR 0003.
