# VAL-DESKTOP-READER: Disciplined desktop reading workspace

Surface: production Chromium at 1440 by 1000 and 200 percent zoom.
Needs: `VAL-READER-SHELL`, `VAL-READER-RESPONSIVE`, `VAL-READER-STYLE`, and the persistent Slice 2 study board.
Behavior: Reader, docked Study, and expanded Study preserve source position and clear hierarchy; all themes cover the workspace; opening/resizing/closing adjacent panels neither obscures controls nor drops selection; source-return restores the cited passage; zoom creates no app-shell overflow.
Evidence: Light/dark/sepia desktop screenshots; docked/expanded geometry; selection and source-location traces across panel transitions; source-return trace; zoom/overflow measurements.
