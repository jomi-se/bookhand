# VAL-MOBILE-PANELS: Coherent Contents, Text, and Study surfaces

Surface: Chromium touch emulation at 412 by 915 and 320 pixels.
Needs: `VAL-READER-RESPONSIVE`, `VAL-READER-ACCESSIBILITY`, and production preview.
Behavior: Each panel replaces the reading surface with exactly one header and no irrelevant reading footer; opening moves focus to the panel heading or first purposeful control; closing by button or Escape restores the invoking control; only one panel is exposed; reader location and selection are preserved.
Evidence: Screenshots and landmark tree; focus trace for every trigger/close path; exposed/hidden accessibility state; location/selection before/after; geometry at both widths.
