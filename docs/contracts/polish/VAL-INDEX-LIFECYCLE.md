# VAL-INDEX-LIFECYCLE: Transactional local lexical index

Surface: storage worker and browser status UI.
Needs: `VAL-MATH-PASSAGE`, `VAL-TEST-CONTROL-INTEGRITY`, official SQLite WASM/FTS5, and the two books pinned in `tests/fixtures/search/polish-oracle.json`.
Behavior: Import or bundled bootstrap creates versioned, book-scoped, CFI-anchored chunks in bounded batches. Each committed batch transaction updates its chunks and synchronized FTS rows together. Replacement/removal cannot leave stale rows; version mismatch reindexes. Cancellation after a committed batch stops before the next batch, and restart reuses every matching-version committed chunk rather than deleting/recomputing it. Injected failure at a named chunk leaves prior batches queryable as explicitly partial and retry resumes from them. Empty, failed, partial, and complete states are truthful; reading remains available after index failure; no book bytes or text leave origin.
Evidence: Test-only worker seams `indexPauseAfterCommittedBatch` and `indexFailBeforeChunk` drive cancellation and failure through the real worker protocol; worker integration covers create/replace/remove/version/pause/failure/resume, committed chunk identities, and chunk/FTS counts after every boundary. Preserve visible state traces, blocked non-origin network log, successful reading during failure, and production exclusion of both seams.
