# VAL-INDEX-LIFECYCLE: Transactional local lexical index

Surface: storage worker and browser status UI.
Needs: `VAL-MATH-PASSAGE`, `VAL-SOURCE-EXCERPT-LIFECYCLE`, official SQLite WASM/FTS5, and the two books pinned in `tests/fixtures/search/polish-oracle.json`.
Behavior: Opening an imported or bundled book for the first time starts versioned, book-scoped, CFI-anchored chunking after the Foliate reader is ready; import alone creates no invented anchors. Chunks commit in batches of at most 250. Each batch transaction updates its chunks, synchronized FTS rows, and exact section/chunk resume cursor together. Replacement/removal cannot leave stale rows. A source-extraction or tokenizer version mismatch performs one atomic transition that clears only that book's retrieval rows, advances its epoch, records the new versions and a zero resume cursor, and leaves the index `partial`; reindexing then proceeds through ordinary committed batches. An explicit Cancel action, book close, or book switch requests cooperative cancellation. The in-flight transaction either commits completely or rolls back completely, and no subsequent batch starts. Reopening or Retry resumes from the committed cursor. Repeated start/Retry requests coalesce so at most one indexing job owns a book at a time. Restart reuses every matching-version committed chunk and identity rather than deleting or recomputing it. Injected failure at a named chunk rolls back that batch, records a bounded failure, leaves prior batches queryable as explicitly partial, and retry resumes from the persisted cursor. No metadata row means the index is `not-started`; a begun, running, or cancelled index is durably `partial`; an indexing error is `failed`; explicit completion is `complete`, including a genuinely text-empty book. Search availability is `unavailable` when no committed chunks exist before completion, `partial` when committed chunks exist but state is not complete, and `ready` after completion. Reading remains mounted and usable in every index state; no book bytes or text leave origin.
Evidence: Test-only worker seams `indexPauseAfterCommittedBatch` and `indexFailBeforeChunk` drive cancellation and failure through the real worker protocol; worker integration covers first-open create, replace/remove/version/cursor/epoch/pause/failure/resume, stable committed chunk identities, one-job ownership, and chunk/FTS counts after every boundary. Browser and worker traces cover cancellation during a batch, close/switch during indexing, repeated Retry, coalescing, and resume after reopening. Browser evidence preserves the pre-index `not-started` UI state and metadata-backed `partial`, `failed`, and `complete` states near retrieval controls, including a genuinely text-empty completed book; the UI must not label `not-started` or text-empty completion as failure. Reading stays usable during failure and the non-origin network log stays empty. State-transition and injected-failure browser evidence runs in the secure `test-harness` preview through the genuine reader, worker, and UI paths. A separate production build/preview proves both seam names and every activation route are absent.
